Biography

An educator who became a psychologist — and a psychologist who never stopped teaching.

"People change when they are understood, not instructed."

For more than thirty years, Dr. Abdussalam Salmani taught at high school and higher secondary levels across Kerala. He spent decades working daily with adolescents — the formative age group where identity, career ambition, peer pressure, and self-doubt collide. That deep, lived classroom experience became the bedrock for everything that followed.

A doctorate in psychology, postgraduate degrees in psychology and human resource management, and fifteen years of clinical consulting turned intuitive classroom wisdom into rigorous scientific method. Alongside clinical casework, Dr. Salmani has served as a Career Guidance Counsellor under Higher Secondary Education, Kerala, Senior Resource Person with the Centre for Information and Guidance India (CIGI Calicut), and District Joint Co-ordinator for Career Guidance & Adolescent Counselling in Malappuram.

Today, Dr. Salmani moves seamlessly between the consulting room, the educational campus, and the international keynote stage — bringing the exact same clarity, empathy, and evidence-based guidance to each setting.

Philosophy & Mission

Understanding first. Guidance second.

Every session, workshop and keynote follows the same principle: people change when they feel understood. Dr. Salmani's mission is to help individuals, families and institutions understand their own minds — and act on that understanding with clarity and confidence.

The same clarity, empathy and evidence-based guidance is brought to the consulting room, the school campus and the training stage.

The Journey

The Journey, in Three Chapters

Each phase was purposefully built upon the one before it — classroom observation led to clinical inquiry, and clinical mastery expanded to international keynote stages.

Chapter One

The Classroom

Three decades teaching at higher secondary level. Mentoring thousands of young learners every year provided an invaluable first-hand education in how human minds develop, process emotion, and respond to encouragement.

Chapter Two

The Consulting Room

A rigorous academic quest culminating in an MSc and a Doctorate in Psychology. Founding his clinical and family counselling practice at Kondotty to address family friction, learning blocks, and mental well-being.

Chapter Three

The Global Stage

Conducted over 4,000 keynote addresses and workshops spanning education, psychology, family dynamics, and human resource management across India and the Middle East, mentoring teachers, parents, and civic leaders.
